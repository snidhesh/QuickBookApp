Journal import for QuickBooks Online

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains what data Journal import for QuickBooks Online (“the app”) handles, where it goes and how long it is kept. BlackOak Real Estate LLC operates the app as an internal tool for its own staff and its own QuickBooks Online company.

Data the app handles

  • The QuickBooks connection. When an administrator connects QuickBooks, the app receives access and refresh tokens from Intuit, plus the QuickBooks company ID and name. These let it act on that one company.
  • Uploaded spreadsheets. The file is read in memory to check the entries and is not saved. What the app keeps for posting is the checked entries: dates, journal numbers, amounts, descriptions, memos, and the QuickBooks IDs of the accounts, names, classes and locations they use.
  • Posting results. For each entry posted: the QuickBooks transaction ID, or the error message QuickBooks returned.
  • QuickBooks lists. To check a file, the app reads the company’s accounts, customers, vendors, employees, classes, locations, currency settings, and existing journal entries for the dates in the file. These are used during the check and are not stored.
  • Sign-in. A cookie that keeps you signed in for 7 days, and a short-lived cookie (10 minutes) that protects the QuickBooks connection step. After a wrong password, the app counts failed attempts from your IP address for 15 minutes to block guessing.

The app doesn’t use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and it doesn’t collect data for any purpose other than posting journal entries.

How long data is kept

  • Checked entries and posting results are deleted automatically one hour after upload.
  • The QuickBooks connection is kept until someone disconnects it, from the app or from QuickBooks. Intuit also ends it after about 100 days without use.
  • Failed sign-in counts are deleted after 15 minutes.
  • Entries posted to QuickBooks stay in QuickBooks, under the company’s own QuickBooks settings and Intuit’s policies.

Who else processes the data

  • Intuit (QuickBooks Online) receives the entries you post and provides the lists the app checks against.
  • Vercel hosts the app. Its servers may be outside the UAE, including in the United States, and it may keep standard request logs for a limited time.
  • Upstash stores the QuickBooks connection and the checked entries for up to one hour, as described above.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface, so your browser requests font files from Google.

Data is not sold or shared with anyone else. All traffic to and from the app uses HTTPS.

Disconnecting

Use Disconnect in the app, or disconnect the app from within QuickBooks. Disconnecting from the app deletes the stored tokens and asks Intuit to revoke them. After that the app can’t read from or post to QuickBooks until an administrator connects it again.
