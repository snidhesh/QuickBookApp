Post journal entries from a spreadsheet

Upload an Excel file. Each entry is checked against your QuickBooks accounts and must balance before it can be posted.

What the file needs

Journal Date
One date per entry.
Account Name
As it appears in QuickBooks, or the account number. Sub-accounts as Parent:Child.
Debits, Credits
One amount per line. Each entry's debits must equal its credits.

Optional: Journal No, Description, Name (customer, vendor or employee), Class, Location, Currency, Journal Memo.

Lines with the same Journal No form one entry. Without a Journal No column, lines are grouped in order until their debits and credits balance.
