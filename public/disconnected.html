Journal import no longer has access to your QuickBooks company. It can’t read your lists or post journal entries until it’s connected again.

Entries already posted stay in QuickBooks. Nothing else needs to be done.

To connect again

Open the app, sign in, and click Connect to QuickBooks. A QuickBooks administrator of the company needs to approve the connection.
